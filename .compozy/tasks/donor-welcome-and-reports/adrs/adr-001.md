# ADR-001: Two complementary donor location reports

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-10-02

## Context

The administrative team needs to understand where benefactors live and which priorado or chapel they attend. These are different relationships and may point to different places for the same person. The operator explicitly requested two charts.

## Decision

Present two separate donor enrollment charts: one grouped by state of residence and one grouped by the selected priorado or chapel. Label both measures as enrollment counts and retain explicit unknown or none categories so totals remain intelligible.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: One chart based on priorado or chapel only

- **Pros:** Uses a value already selected during enrollment.
- **Cons:** Does not show where benefactors live.
- **Why rejected:** It does not meet the requested residence view.

### Alternative 2: One combined chart

- **Pros:** Occupies less dashboard space.
- **Cons:** Conflates residence with religious attendance and obscures differences between them.
- **Why rejected:** It makes the two questions harder to answer accurately.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Staff can answer both geographic questions independently.
- Unknown residence data remains visible rather than being attributed to a chapel location.

### Negative

- The reporting page needs room for two visualizations and their accessible data views.

### Risks

- Historical records may have incomplete residence information; the report must display that gap explicitly.

Technical treatment of historical residence data belongs in the TechSpec.
