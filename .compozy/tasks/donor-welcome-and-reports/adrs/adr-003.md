# ADR-003: Public explanation sourced from the institutional presentation

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-10-02

## Context

Potential benefactors need to understand the Arca before enrolling. The operator requested an item on the main public site, outside the administrative area, and designated `docs/examples/presentation_ansp.pdf` as the governing source for its facts and text. The presentation is a slide deck, while the website needs a concise, accessible explanation.

## Decision

Add a discoverable "Como funciona" entry to the public navigation. Explain why stable scholarship funding is needed, how support flows through the association to schools and students, and what benefactors, the association, schools, and families each do. Lead readers to the existing benefactor signup path. Adapt the presentation's language for the web while keeping substantive claims traceable to its slides.

The operator decided that the page should not mention the income thresholds, scholarship percentages, child-order rules, or complementary eligibility conditions in slides 15–16. The operator also clarified that FSSPX affiliation is not mandatory, so the page must not present it as a requirement. Omit named staff, undated figures, and possible outcomes unless separately verified as current.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Link directly to the PDF

- **Pros:** Keeps the source intact with little editorial work.
- **Cons:** The slide deck is harder to scan and navigate on a phone and does not provide a clear path to enrollment.
- **Why rejected:** It does not provide the requested explanation on the main site.

### Alternative 2: Put the explanation in the administrative area

- **Pros:** Uses an existing application area.
- **Cons:** Potential benefactors cannot use a private page to decide whether to participate.
- **Why rejected:** The operator explicitly requested a public item.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Visitors can understand the project and proceed to enrollment from the public site.
- Content review can trace each substantive claim to the presentation or a separately approved current source.

### Negative

- The explanation requires editorial review when institutional criteria or operations change.

### Risks

- Copying eligibility details from slides 15–16 could mislead applicants and benefactors; the public explanation excludes them.
- Undated presentation figures and named roles can become stale.
