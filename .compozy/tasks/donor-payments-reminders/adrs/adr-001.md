# ADR-001: Stage the donor journey and due-day rule

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-10-26

## Context

The donor form asks for a one-time receipt before the visitor sees the Pix key and QR code. Monthly pledges store a reminder preference but no payment day. Existing monthly rows need a usable day before reminders can run.

## Decision

Display the existing Pix beneficiary, key, QR code, bank, and CNPJ next to the payment choice. For `unica`, follow those instructions with payment date and a required receipt. For `mensal`, collect `dia_vencimento` from 1 through 28 and show no receipt input; the server discards any monthly receipt fields. Backfill existing monthly rows from the `created_at` day in `America/Sao_Paulo`, capped at 28, and let Administrador edit the day.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Keep Pix instructions below the form

- **Pros**: Minimal layout change.
- **Cons**: A one-time donor cannot satisfy the required receipt before discovering the payment destination.
- **Why rejected**: The sequence blocks the intended journey.

### Alternative 2: Infer monthly payment day from `data_pagamento`

- **Pros**: No new column.
- **Cons**: The field is populated only for one-time pledges and represents a full payment date, not a recurring day.
- **Why rejected**: It has no valid monthly meaning.

## Consequences

### Positive

- One-time donors can complete the Pix before uploading its evidence.
- Monthly schedules have a predictable day without requiring historical donor contact.

### Negative

- Existing monthly donors receive an inferred initial day that administrators may need to correct.

### Risks

- Duplicated Pix details could drift. Render one shared component or data source in the revised page.

## Implementation Notes

Add a database constraint for monthly `dia_vencimento` in 1–28 and null for `unica`. Apply the backfill in the migration before enforcing the constraint. Preserve `data_pagamento` only for `unica`.

## Amendment

[ADR-006](adr-006.md) supersedes the public 1–28 input, monthly on-screen Pix requirement, and 1–28 constraint for new work. The completed 1–28 migration/backfill remains historical; the new migration permits 1–31 for monthly rows, and the public form accepts only 1, 10, 15, 20, or 30. The one-time Pix → payment date → receipt sequence remains in force.
