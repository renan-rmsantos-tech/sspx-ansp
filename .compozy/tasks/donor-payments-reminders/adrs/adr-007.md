# ADR-007: Require a receipt for every monthly occurrence

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-10-26

## Context

The completed phase-2 reminder and public receipt flow treats monthly receipts as optional evidence. The operator now requires one receipt for each monthly payment, while registration remains receipt-free and bank-statement review remains the only proof of received funds. The signed receipt page is available without login, and the new monthly instructions email can provide the first occurrence's link.

## Decision

Derive `Comprovante pendente` for every monthly ledger occurrence until its own valid receipt is uploaded. Then show `Comprovante enviado – aguardando conferência` while bank review is pending; preserve bank status as a separate dimension. Require the first-occurrence signed link in the monthly instructions email and each occurrence's signed link in reminders. Update the public form, default and rendered email copy, and receipt page to request the mandatory per-payment receipt, while keeping registration free of upload. An uploaded receipt suppresses all remaining 10-day/5-day reminders for that occurrence, including manual retries; it does not suppress another month's reminder or imply bank confirmation. A valid receipt may be uploaded or replaced after a bank credit was confirmed or marked divergent, preserving its bank status and amount. For donors who declined reminders, Administrador can manually send or resend instructions with a link scoped to a selected pending occurrence; an expired standard link can be renewed for 14 days through that authenticated action. The admin donor list shows the current occurrence's receipt state. Phase-3 conference, CSV, and report distinguish receipt pending/sent from `Confirmado no extrato`, `Divergente`, and `Não identificado`, and highlight overdue occurrences without receipts.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Require a receipt during registration

- **Pros**: Collects evidence immediately.
- **Cons**: The first payment occurs after the explanatory email and may not exist at registration.
- **Why rejected**: It blocks the intended monthly sign-up journey.

### Alternative 2: Continue reminders after a receipt is uploaded

- **Pros**: Simpler sender selection.
- **Cons**: The reminder asks for evidence the donor already provided.
- **Why rejected**: It creates confusing duplicate requests.

### Alternative 3: Treat a receipt upload as bank payment confirmation

- **Pros**: Fewer visible states.
- **Cons**: A receipt file does not prove a credit in ANSP's bank account.
- **Why rejected**: It would misstate received funds.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Each occurrence has a clear evidence obligation and a truthful bank-review state.
- Reminder traffic stops for an occurrence once its requested receipt arrives.
- Administrators can find overdue missing receipts without conflating them with bank debt.

### Negative

- Completed reminder, signed upload, and receipt-page code need follow-up changes in new phase-2 tasks.
- Existing admin-edited reminder copy may need a fixed required-receipt sentence at rendering time.

### Risks

- A receipt can arrive between reminder selection and provider call. Recheck receipt state immediately before the provider call; a send already accepted by Resend remains historical.
- Expired signed links can leave a required receipt pending. Keep the pending state visible and let only Administrador issue a scoped 14-day recovery link through an explicit manual instructions resend; never expose a public donor lookup or expiry override.

## Implementation Notes

See TechSpec “Receipt-required amendment,” “Reminder Reservation and Send Flow,” and “Conference, Totals, and Export Contract.” Implement via new tasks after task_17; retain completed task statuses. Do not add a second mutable receipt-state column when the active receipt metadata already determines pending/sent.
