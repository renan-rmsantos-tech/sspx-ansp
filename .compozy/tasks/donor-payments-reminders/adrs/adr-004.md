# ADR-004: Reconcile manually against bank-confirmed credits

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-10-26

## Context

The app records pledges and one-time receipt files but no bank-confirmed payment. ANSP reviews Pix credits manually. A receipt or pledge cannot establish that money reached the account, and an import flow is outside this delivery.

## Decision

Keep one ledger row per pledge and competence, containing expected amount, recorded received amount, credit date, state, confirming administrator and time, and note. A one-time pledge gets one pending row linked to its existing receipt. An administrator explicitly records a bank credit; equal values become `confirmado`, unequal positive values become `divergente`. `nao_identificado` means a reviewed expected item with no matching credit. Use the ledger for totals, overdue items, CSV, and charts. Materialize legacy monthly rows from the launch competence forward and label the coverage boundary; never infer prelaunch bank receipts.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Treat uploaded receipts as paid

- **Pros**: Automatic one-time completion.
- **Cons**: A file does not prove settlement in ANSP's bank account.
- **Why rejected**: It overstates received funds.

### Alternative 2: Import bank OFX or CSV immediately

- **Pros**: Could reduce manual entry.
- **Cons**: Bank formats, matching errors, and exception review add a separate integration project.
- **Why rejected**: The operator explicitly deferred bank-statement import.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Expected and received funds are distinct and auditable by confirmer and timestamp.
- The same ledger supports conference, reminders, and accessible reports.

### Negative

- Administrators must enter confirmed credits manually.
- Historical totals before launch are unavailable without later backfill from actual bank statements.

### Risks

- Corrections to amounts can change totals. Validate each edit, retain confirmer metadata, and show divergence clearly.

## Implementation Notes

Use integer cents or `numeric(12,2)` throughout calculations; avoid floating-point totals. Protect all reads, writes, and export with `requireAdmin()`. Reports and CSV omit CPF, full address, email, and receipt URL.
