# ADR-002: Anchor monthly occurrences at registration

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-10-26

## Context

The operator defined registration as the monthly plan's start and `um_ano` as 12 monthly occurrences. A selected day can precede the registration day within the same calendar month, so an unqualified “registration month” rule would create an already-past first due date.

## Decision

Interpret `created_at` in `America/Sao_Paulo` as the start date. The first occurrence is the first selected `dia_vencimento` on or after that date. Subsequent occurrences are on that day in consecutive calendar months. `um_ano` has indexes 0–11, exactly 12 occurrences; `indeterminado` has no index limit. Store the due month as competence `YYYY-MM`, and use calendar arithmetic rather than adding a fixed number of days. Never issue a reminder whose scheduled date precedes registration.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Always use the registration month

- **Pros**: A simple month count.
- **Cons**: A chosen day earlier than registration would be overdue before the pledge existed.
- **Why rejected**: It creates impossible first reminders.

### Alternative 2: Add 30-day intervals from registration

- **Pros**: Simple timestamp arithmetic.
- **Cons**: Dates drift across months and no longer match the chosen calendar day.
- **Why rejected**: It breaks the promised payment day.

## Consequences

### Positive

- A one-year plan has 12 predictable due dates and no retroactive first due date.
- Competence and reminder keys are deterministic.

### Negative

- If a donor chooses a day earlier than their registration day, the first payment is due in the following month.

### Risks

- UTC/local date conversion can shift the registration day. Test dates near midnight UTC and month boundaries.

## Implementation Notes

Use a pure recurrence helper shared by reminder selection, donor-list next due, ledger generation, and report grouping. Snapshot due dates in the ledger once materialized; subsequent day edits update only open future occurrences.

## Amendment

[ADR-006](adr-006.md) extends the first-on-or-after-registration rule to nominal days 29–31. For each competence, the actual due day is capped at that month's last day; 10-day and 5-day reminder dates use this adjusted date. The stored nominal day remains unchanged.
